 

## `metrics.txt` – CryptoBrainNews Data Platform: Product Specification

**Goal:** To build the definitive, institutional-grade data terminal for cryptocurrency markets, on-chain activity, DeFi, and the emerging agentic economy. This document outlines the complete product vision for the `/data` section of CryptoBrainNews.

The following metrics are organized by market vertical and represent the target state for the platform. For each metric, we will identify a reliable data source, a suitable visualization method, and a caching strategy.

---

### 1. Markets
This section aggregates data from centralized and decentralized trading venues to provide a complete view of global market activity.

#### 1.1 Spot
This section will provide granular visibility into the cash market, focusing on the following metrics:
- **Global Aggregate Volumes:**
    - Cryptocurrency Monthly Exchange Volume (Total and by asset)
    - Daily Exchange Volume (7-Day Moving Average)
    - USD- Denominated Exchange Volume (Total and by asset)
    - BTC & ETH Total Exchange Volume (7DMA)
- **Market Structure & Share:**
    - Share of Trade Volume by Pair Denomination (e.g., USD, USDT, BTC, ETH)
    - Monthly Exchange Volume Market Share (e.g., Binance, Coinbase, Kraken)
    - Monthly Spot Pairs for Exchanges (Most active pairs)
    - Spot Volume by Asset (Dominance of top assets)
    - Spot Volume Share by Asset (Comparative analysis)
- **Regional & Derivative Flows:**
    - Asia-Based, Europe-Based, North America-Based, South America-Based Customer Exchange Volume
    - BTC/EUR Volumes (in BTC)
    - ETH/EUR Volumes (in ETH)
    - BTC Spot to Futures Volume (30DMA)
    - ETH Spot to Futures Volume (30DMA)
- **Exchange-Specific & Advanced Metrics:**
    - Share of BUSD / TUSD / FDUSD Trading on Binance
    - Binance Share of Volume by Fee Type (e.g., VIP, Taker, Maker)

#### 1.2 Futures & Perpetuals
Data on derivatives markets, providing insights into sentiment, leverage, and institutional positioning.
- **Open Interest (OI):**
    - Aggregated Open Interest of Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana Futures (in USD and native units)
    - Share of Open Interest across Bitcoin Futures (by exchange)
    - Top 5 Bitcoin / Ether Futures Open Interest Exchanges
    - Futures Open Interest by Asset (Total and per asset)
    - CME Futures: Volume, OI, and Large Holder Report (COTs)
- **Volume & Liquidations:**
    - Volume of Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana Futures
    - Total Futures Volumes (Aggregate and by asset)
    - BTC & ETH Futures Volume (7DMA)
    - Futures Volume by Asset (Comparative)
    - BTC & ETH Liquidations (Long, Short, and Total Number)
    - Hyperliquid Total Cumulative Liquidations and Daily Liquidations
- **Funding & Basis:**
    - BTC & ETH Funding Rates (7DMA, APY)
    - BTC Annualized Daily Basis (Binance)
    - BTC Long/Short Ratio on Binance (Accounts)
    - FTT Average Funding Rate
- **Advanced Metrics:**
    - APT Perpetual Volume
    - FTT Futures Volumes
    - FTXDXY-PERP Volume and Open Interest
    - Coinbase International Daily Volume
    - Volume and OI of CME Solana futures

#### 1.3 Crypto Indices
Proprietary and third-party indices for tracking sector-specific performance.
- GMCI Indices: Price and Performance data for:
    - GM30 (Top 30), GML1 (Layer 1), GML2 (Layer 2), GMMEME (Memecoins), GMDEFI (DeFi), GMDEPIN (DePIN), GMGM (Gaming), GMAI (AI), GMSMALL (Small Cap), GMMID (Mid Cap), GMUSA (US Tokens)
- Ecosystem Indices: GMSOL (Solana Select), GMETH (Ethereum Select), GMSOLMEME (Solana Memecoins), GMSOLEX (Solana ex-Memecoins)
- Price Performance of GMCI Indices (1D, 7D, 30D, YTD, 1Y)

#### 1.4 CME COTs (Commitment of Traders)
Institutional positioning data from the Chicago Mercantile Exchange.
- Large Open Interest Holders for Bitcoin, Ether, and Micro Bitcoin Futures
- Open Interest by Trader Category (Long/Short): Producer/Merchant/Processor/User, Swap Dealers, Managed Money, Other Reportables
- Net Positions by Trader Category

#### 1.5 Options
Data on volatility and institutional hedging activity.
- **Aggregated Metrics:**
    - Aggregated Open Interest & Volume of Bitcoin and Ethereum Options
    - Share of Open Interest Across Bitcoin and Ethereum Options
    - Open Interest Put/Call Ratio
- **Deribit (Leading Exchange):**
    - Bitcoin & Ether Options Open Interest (in BTC/ETH) by Expiry
- **CME Options:**
    - Volume and OI of CME Bitcoin and Ethereum Options
- **Volatility Metrics:**
    - BTC & ETH DVol Index vs Spot, Vol of Vol, Variance Premium
    - BTC & ETH ATM Implied Volatility
    - BTC & ETH Option Skew (Delta 5, 25)

#### 1.6 Prices
Core pricing and market health indicators.
- **Core Market Health:**
    - Crypto Total Market Cap
    - Bitcoin & Ethereum Dominance
    - Fear and Greed Index
- **Asset Prices:**
    - BTC Price, ETH Price, and prices for all major L1s and DeFi tokens
- **Performance & Correlation:**

- **Other:**

#### 1.9 Sports Tokens
